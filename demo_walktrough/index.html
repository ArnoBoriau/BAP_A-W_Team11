Fenestra

Concept walkthrough

  • W A S D or arrow keys to walk
  • Mouse to look around (click the view first)
  • Shift to run
  • E to use the big screen when you stand close to it
  • 1 · 2 · 3 to jump to Hall A, the entrance or Hall B
  • R to restart the demo
  • Esc to free your mouse or close a screen
